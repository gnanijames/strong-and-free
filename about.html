My Story

I built the program I couldn't find anywhere else.

I'm Gnani. I'm a strength and embodiment coach based in Markham, Toronto — and I started Strong and Free out of frustration.

Over the years, I kept watching the same pattern play out. Adults in their 50s, 60s, and 70s would come to me after years of being told their bodies were just "getting old." They'd been handed generic programs, ignored by fitness studios designed for 25-year-olds, or told to "take it easy."

They weren't fragile. They weren't done. They just hadn't been given the right support.

Strong and Free is built on a simple belief: movement is medicine, and it's never too late. The research backs this up — strength training in your 50s, 60s, and 70s improves not just your body, but your mood, your memory, your balance, and your independence.

My job is to make that accessible. No gym required. No intimidating equipment. Just expert coaching, a supportive community, and a program that actually fits your life.

Strength first

Designed for 55+
