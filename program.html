The Program

Move Strong and Free Group Classes

Live, expert-led fitness sessions designed specifically for adults 55+. Join from home, on any device — no app, no gym, no guesswork.

Every Session

What happens in a class


  • Warm-up (10 min)

    Joint mobility and gentle activation — protects your body and gets you ready to move safely.

  • Strength work (25–30 min)

    Functional exercises using bodyweight, chairs, and light household items. No special equipment needed.

  • Balance and coordination (10 min)

    Balance training is one of the most important things you can do to prevent falls and stay independent.

  • Cool-down and stretch (10 min)

    Guided recovery to reduce soreness and keep you moving well into the next session.

Common Questions

Everything you want to know

Do I need any equipment?

No. Every class uses bodyweight exercises and everyday items — a sturdy chair, a wall, and optionally a light household object. Nothing to buy.

What do I need to join online?

Any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera and internet connection. Google Meet runs in your browser — there's no app to download. We'll send you a simple guide in your welcome email.

What if I miss a class?

Life happens. Your subscription runs month to month regardless of attendance. We do not currently offer recorded replays, but this is something we're working toward for members.

I have a health condition or injury. Is this safe?

We recommend consulting your doctor before starting any new exercise program. Move Strong and Free classes are designed to be low-impact and adaptable, but Gnani is not a physician. If you have specific concerns, book a consultation so Gnani can review your situation before you join the group.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel any time through your Stripe customer portal — a link is included in every email we send you. No phone calls, no forms, no waiting periods. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing month.

Can I gift this to a parent or family member?

Yes — and this is one of the most thoughtful gifts you can give. When checking out, you can enter a different name and email for the recipient. They'll receive the welcome email directly. Contact us if you need help setting this up.
